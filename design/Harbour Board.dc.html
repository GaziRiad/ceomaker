Harbour · Free
A warm greeting and a large arched portrait on soft white.
For leaders who want to come across as personal and easy to reach.

Type · Figtree only

Desktop / phone. Name h1: 300, 96 / 54 px (76 / 44 if over 24 characters), +16 / +6 with no hero image, then capped at container ÷ (longest unbroken part × 0.56). Line height 1.02, tracking −0.025em. Greeting 500, 20 / 17. Headline 400, 28 / 21, line height 1.3. Section h2 300, 48 / 34. Quote 400, 27 / 21. Item titles 500, 21–23. Body 400, 18 / 17, line height 1.6–1.7. Labels 500, 14–15. Small section labels (Achievements, Gallery) 500, 15, caps, 0.08em.

No mid-word breaks: overflow-wrap normal, hyphens manual. Emails and URLs may wrap anywhere.

Derived colours

Owner sets bg, ink, ac. Each derived value is a color-mix; the percentage is found by stepping until the contrast target is met against every ground it sits on.

  • sf = ink 4 % into bg (tinted bands). sf2 = ink 7 % (cards, portrait band).
  • tint = accent 16 % into bg (call-to-action panel).
  • ink2 = ink into bg from 50 %, up until ≥ 4.6:1 on bg, sf, sf2 and tint.
  • br (inputs, link pills) = ink into bg, ≥ 3.1:1. rule = ink 12 % (decorative lines only).
  • act (small accent text) = accent toward ink until ≥ 4.6:1 on all grounds.
  • acg (large figures, quote mark, focus ring) = accent toward ink until ≥ 3:1.
  • Buttons: ink fill, bg text; hover fills act.

Images

  • Hero portrait 4:5, arch (top radius half the width), 440 px desktop, up to 300 px phone. Tinted band behind its lower 44 %.
  • About 4:5, radius 24. Portfolio 3:2 on top of the card. Gallery 1:1, radius 20. Quote photo 48 px circle.
  • All crops object-fit cover at the owner's focal point. Tinted = grayscale + accent multiply at 35 %; Mono = grayscale.
  • Missing image: hero turns typographic; About becomes one centred column; portfolio cards stay text-only; quotes show the name only; Gallery with no photos is hidden.
  • Gallery per row: desktop up to 6 (never fewer than 3 slots wide); 7–12 use 6, 4, 5 or 3, whichever divides evenly, else 4 with the last row centred. Phone 2.

Motion · CSS only

Fade and rise 24 px, cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). Hero on load, 0.9 s, portrait +160 ms. Sections and items rise on scroll with animation-timeline: view(), range entry 0–30 %, inside @supports and prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference. Without support or JavaScript, everything is simply visible.

Layout and choices

  • Container breakpoints on the root (container-type: inline-size): phone < 640, tablet 640–999, desktop ≥ 1000. Inline nav at desktop when 7 items or fewer, otherwise a Menu pill and full-screen sheet.
  • Sections render in the owner's order. Focus and Testimonials sit on the sf band, never two bands in a row.
  • Free plan: no form, so email and links fill the right-hand card. Pro: they move under the blurb and the form takes the card. Nothing fixed on the page; the footer keeps 96 px below its last line so the platform pill never covers it.
  • The greeting is template wording ("Hello, I'm", up to 40 characters). An eyebrow, if set, replaces it.
  • New content: none needed.
  • The Sofia screenshots weren't in the project, so the structure follows the live demo and the Linen palette is an approximation of its white, charcoal and soft rose.
  • Rounded cards and pill buttons keep it warm and clearly apart from Meridian's square serif masthead.

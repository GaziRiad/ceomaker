Built for executives in fashion, media, design, hospitality, architecture and consumer brands, and for speakers, authors and consumer investors. A dark gallery ground, Italiana set large and uppercase, Hanken Grotesk for reading, thin corner brackets around photos and buttons, and a collage that forms from three photos up. Every frame below is the live template. All copy belongs to a fictional owner, Elena Marlowe. The dashed tiles are image slots standing in for photos. Drag a photo onto any of them. Slots share ids across frames (sl-portrait, sl-g1 to sl-g12, sl-about, sl-w1 to sl-w12, sl-q1 to sl-q10), so a photo dropped once fills every frame after a reload. On a live site an empty slot never renders: the layout closes up instead.

Your name in a large serif, hung salon-style among your own photographs. For leaders whose work is seen as much as read.
Why Salon A salon hang is the way galleries have shown many pictures on one wall for three centuries: frames of different sizes arranged around a centre. That is what the hero does around the owner's name. The word is short, works in English, French and Portuguese, and sits alongside Meridian and Monument without describing a profession. Line 1 describes what you see. Line 2 says who it suits. Both stay under 80 characters for the picker card.

Folio

Your work as large projects people can swipe through, with a clear story around them. For founders, investors and operators whose career is best shown as projects. A warm paper ground, Geist set large with tight tracking, Geist Mono for kinds, years and counters, soft-cornered cards, and a full-bleed work carousel at the centre. It follows the reference's order and spacing: floating pill header, name beside portrait, about, figures, focus cards, career list, work carousel, a dark closing panel. Apart from Meridian (quiet serif), Monument (condensed type on colour) and Salon (dark gallery, serif collage), Folio is a light, project-first grotesk layout. Every frame below is the live template; all copy belongs to a fictional owner, Maya Lindqvist. Dashed tiles are image slots. Drop a photo on any of them; double-click a dropped photo to pan and scale it, which stands in for the owner's focal point. Slots share ids across frames (fo-portrait, fo-about, fo-g1–12, fo-w1–12, fo-q1–10), so a photo dropped once fills every frame after a reload. On a live site an empty slot never renders.

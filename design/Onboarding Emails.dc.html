Button · one style, emails 1, 2, 4

  • Background #416180, label #ffffff, square corners, no border or shadow.
  • Label 15/18 bold uppercase, 0.08em, ending in " →" (a character, not an icon).
  • Padding 15 × 28, so 48px tall. Desktop: auto width, left aligned.
  • Phone: 100% width, label centred, 335 × 48 tap target.
  • If the editor can't change width per breakpoint, use 100% everywhere.
  • Secondary actions are bold underlined text links in #416180.

Decisions

  • Icon, not the lockup. The lockup's "CEO" is ink on transparent and disappears when a client darkens the ground. The icon is a solid accent tile with a white mark, so it reads on light and dark. Host: www.ceomaker.app/brand/ceomaker-icon-120.png at 40×40.
  • Flat and mostly text. One ground colour, no cards or bands: fewer fills for dark mode to invert, and it reads as product mail rather than a newsletter.
  • Pictures sit in a 1px frame (a section with 1px padding) so they keep an edge on any template colour. With images off the frame keeps its size and shows the alt text; give the image a #e9e9ea background and 14px #5d5d60 alt styling. The address and caption carry the meaning in text.
  • Email 1 is complete without its picture. Nothing in the copy refers to it; remove the section when there's no URL.
  • The computer prompt sits right above the button in email 1, and right below it in email 2, where people decide whether to tap now.
  • Riad signs email 1, so email 3 comes from someone they've already heard from.
  • Draft links go to www.ceomaker.app/dashboard (sign-in if needed, then the draft), because personalisation has no site id. If one becomes available, deep-link to /dashboard/sites/{id}/edit.
  • Dark mode. If Resend allows head CSS, add <meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark"> and a prefers-color-scheme block with the dark values. Gmail ignores it and inverts on its own; the icon, the #416180 button with white label and the mid-grey dividers survive either path.
  • Not included: a postal address in the footer. Add one if your sending region requires it. The pictures show sample content (Helen Okafor, Meridian template).

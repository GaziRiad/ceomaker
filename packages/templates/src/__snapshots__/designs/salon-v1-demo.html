About

I joined Meridian as COO in 2015, when it was a regional carrier with thin margins and no shared planning system. Six years later we run one of Europe's most reliable mid-sized freight networks.

Before Meridian, I spent a decade in operations at Alder & Crane Logistics in Hamburg. I sit on the board of the European Freight Council and mentor first-time founders in industrial technology.
